## Set up in five steps

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   <span class="tj-icon">:lucide-brain:</span> **1. Connect an AI model**

    ---

    Add your OpenAI, Gemini, Claude or OpenRouter API key. This is the brain that talks to your leads.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: AI models](setup/ai-models.md)

-   <span class="tj-icon">:lucide-radio:</span> **2. Connect a channel**

    ---

    WhatsApp, Telegram, Email or SMS — where the AI messages your leads.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Channels](setup/channels/index.md)

-   <span class="tj-icon">:lucide-users:</span> **3. Add human agents**

    ---

    Teammates who take over when a lead is ready to buy or needs a person.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Human agents](setup/human-agents.md)

-   <span class="tj-icon">:lucide-scan-eye:</span> **4. Create a nurture campaign**

    ---

    One campaign per product: knowledge base, tone, channels and follow-ups.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Nurture campaigns](nurture-campaigns/create-a-campaign.md)

-   <span class="tj-icon">:lucide-puzzle:</span> **5. Connect lead sources**

    ---

    Meta Lead Ads and Google Ads forms send new leads in automatically.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Lead sources](setup/lead-sources/index.md)

-   <span class="tj-icon">:lucide-bot-message-square:</span> **Watch it work**

    ---

    Follow live conversations, take over any chat and hand it back to the AI.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Conversations](engage/conversations.md)

</div>
