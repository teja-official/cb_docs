# Setup

Everything a nurture campaign needs before it can talk to leads lives under **Setup** in the sidebar. Admins (and roles with the matching *manage* permission) can change these pages; everyone else sees them read-only or not at all.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   <span class="tj-icon">:lucide-brain:</span> **[AI models](ai-models.md)**

    ---

    Connect OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, OpenRouter and more with your own API key.

-   <span class="tj-icon">:lucide-radio:</span> **[Channels](channels/index.md)**

    ---

    WhatsApp, Telegram, Email and SMS (Twilio) — where conversations happen.

-   <span class="tj-icon">:lucide-users:</span> **[Human agents](human-agents.md)**

    ---

    Team members who take over from the AI, with routing priority, capacity and alerts.

-   <span class="tj-icon">:lucide-puzzle:</span> **[Lead sources](lead-sources/index.md)**

    ---

    Meta Ads, Google Ads, Google Sheets and spreadsheet uploads.

</div>

!!! info "Plan limits"
    Your plan sets how many AI models, channels, agents and lead forms you can have, and which channel types are available. See what you're using in **Account → Billing → Plan Usage**. When a limit allows only **one active** AI model or channel, activating another one asks you to confirm switching over to it.
