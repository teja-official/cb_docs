# Choose a plan

[[ app.name ]] has **no free trial**. A new workspace has no plan, and the app unlocks as soon as the owner pays. Compare plans at <[[ app.plans_url ]]>.

## What works before you pay

| Area | Without a plan |
|---|---|
| **Team & Workspace** | :lucide-check: Open — rename the workspace, manage members and invitations |
| **Profile, Account settings, Help & Support** | :lucide-check: Open |
| **Billing** (Subscription, Plan Usage, Payment History, Referrals) | :lucide-check: Open — so you can pay |
| Dashboard, Nurture Campaigns, Conversations, Leads, AI Models, Channels, Human Agents, Lead Sources, Settings | :lucide-lock: Locked (shown with a lock in the sidebar) |

Inviting new members and creating custom roles also need an active plan.

!!! info "Invited members"
    If you're a member of someone else's workspace and it has no active plan, you'll see a notice asking you to contact the workspace owner. Only the owner can buy or renew the plan.

## Buy a plan

<div class="steps" markdown>

1. Go to **Account → Billing → Subscription** (or click **Choose a plan** on the notice).
2. Pick a plan and a billing cycle (monthly or yearly).
3. On checkout, check your details. A **welcome offer** is pre-applied if you're eligible — you can remove it and enter a different coupon instead. Only **one coupon** can be used per order.
4. Click pay and complete the payment in the secure **Razorpay** window.
5. You're taken to the payment-success page and the whole app unlocks.

</div>

## Good to know

- **Auto-renew is off until you turn it on** — no surprise charges. Turn it on from **Billing → Subscription** if you want the plan to renew automatically.
- **Cancel anytime.** You keep access until your paid period ends.
- **Upgrade any time.** The unused part of what you already paid is credited toward the new plan.
- **Discounts are capped.** A coupon can take at most 50% off, referral benefits (referral discount and referral credit) at most 30%, and all discounts together at most 50%.
- **Short grace period.** If a plan ends, access continues for a short grace period (2 days) before the app locks.

Everything about subscriptions, usage and invoices is in [Billing & plans](../account/billing.md).
