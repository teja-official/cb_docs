# Create your account

Sign up at <[[ app.register_url ]]>. It takes about a minute.

## Sign up with email

<div class="steps" markdown>

1. **Your details** — enter your first name, last name, email and phone number. If someone referred you, open their referral link — the code is applied from the link (there's no field to type one). The page shows what the code gives you — and warns you if it won't apply because the person who referred you is on the team you're joining (see [Referrals](../account/referrals.md#referrals-dont-apply-between-team-members)).
2. **Verify email** — we email you a verification code. Enter it and click **Verify email**.
3. **Set password** — choose a password and click **Create account**.
4. **Choose a plan** — your account is ready. Owners pick a plan next; see [Choose a plan](choose-a-plan.md).

</div>

!!! note "Use a real, long-term email"
    Throwaway or temporary email addresses are blocked at sign-up. Billing emails, reports and alerts go to this address (you can change the reporting email later in [Account settings](../account/account-settings.md)).

## Sign up with Google or Meta

On the sign-up or login page, click **Google** or **Meta** under the form. A pop-up asks you to sign in and allow access; once you approve, you're signed in.

!!! tip "Nothing happens when you click?"
    Allow pop-ups for [[ app.name ]] in your browser and try again.

## Joining a teammate's workspace

If a teammate invited you, you'll get an email invitation.

- **No account yet?** Open the link in the email. The sign-up page opens with your invitation; after creating your account you land straight in your teammate's workspace — no plan needed, the workspace owner pays.
- **Already have an account?** Open the link and sign in, or accept it later in **Team & Workspace → Invitations for you**.

You can belong to several workspaces and switch between them with the workspace switcher at the top left. See [Team & roles](../workspace/team-and-roles.md).

## Signing in

Go to <[[ app.login_url ]]> and sign in with your email and password, or with Google / Meta.

- **Two-step login** — if you turned it on, we email you a **sign-in code** after your password. Enter it to finish signing in. Turn it on or off in [Account settings → Security](../account/account-settings.md#security).
- **Forgot your password?** Click the reset link on the login page. We email you a code to set a new one.
- **Where you land** — owners without an active plan land on **Billing**. Everyone else lands on their dashboard, or on the page and workspace chosen in [Account settings](../account/account-settings.md#region-format-and-start-page).
