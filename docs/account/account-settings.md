# Account settings

Personal settings that follow you across devices. No permission needed — they only affect you.

<span class="tj-path">Account › **Account Settings**</span>

## My agent profile

Shown if you're a [human agent](../setup/human-agents.md) in the current workspace. *How handoffs reach you in this workspace. Your routing priority and capacity are set by an admin.*

- **Available for handoffs** — turn off to pause new assignments, e.g. when you're away.
- **Alert channel** and its contact (Telegram Chat ID, email or mobile number) — see [Notification channels](../setup/human-agents.md#notification-channels) for how to get each one.

## Emails

About the workspace **you own**. Workspaces you were invited to email their own owner.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Reporting email** | Error alerts, reports and billing emails go here. Defaults to your login email. |
| **Error alerts** | Emails when an AI model, channel, lead source or follow-up fails, so you can fix it quickly. Choose which types: *AI Model Error*, *AI Token Limit*, *Channel Error*, *Meta Connection Error*, *Integration Sync Error*, *Follow-up Not Sent*. |
| **Repeat the same alert** | While an error keeps happening, at most one email per this interval. |
| **Daily workspace report** | Yesterday's leads, conversations and handoffs. |
| **Missed activity report** | End-of-day list of messages and handoffs nobody picked up (on plans that include it). |
| **Leads ready to buy** | When a lead who showed buying interest ends a conversation. The in-app alert is always shown. |
| **Subscription reminders** | A reminder before your plan ends. |
| **Referral rewards** | When someone you referred earns you a reward. |

## In-app notifications

What appears in the bell, in this and every workspace you use. See [Notifications](notifications.md).

## Appearance

*Saved to your account and applied every time you sign in.*

- **Theme** — Light, Dark or System.
- **Start with the sidebar collapsed** — more room for conversations and tables. You can still expand it any time.

## Region, format and start page

*How dates and times are shown to you.*

| Setting | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Timezone** | Used to show every date and time in the app. |
| **Date format** | e.g. day-month-year or month-day-year. |
| **Clock** | 12-hour or 24-hour. |
| **Page after login** | Used when your role can open that page; otherwise your dashboard. |
| **Workspace after login** | Used while that workspace has an active plan. |

## Security

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Password** → **Change password** | We email you a code to set a new password. |
| **Two-step login** | After your password, we email you a sign-in code to finish logging in. Turning it on or off asks for your **Current password**. |
| **Log out of all devices** | Signs you out everywhere, this browser included. Use it if you think someone else has access to your account. |
