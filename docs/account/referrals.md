# Referrals

Share [[ app.name ]] and you both save.

<span class="tj-path">Account › Billing › **Referrals**</span>

## How it works

<div class="steps" markdown>

1. Copy your **referral link** from the Referrals page. Everyone has one.
2. Your friend signs up with your **referral link** — the code is applied automatically. Share the link, not just the code: there's no field to type a code.
3. They get a **discount on their first paid purchase**.
4. When they pay for the first time, you **both earn a reward** — credit toward a future purchase.

</div>

## Your stats

- **Total Uses** — sign-ups with your code.
- **Conversions** — of those, how many paid.
- **Claimable Rewards** — credit ready to use. It's applied at your next checkout.

## Rules

- A code can only be applied **when signing up** with the referral link. It can't be added to an existing account.
- Referral credit covers up to **10%** of an order.
- **Referral limit: 30% per payment.** The referral discount and referral credit together take at most **30%** off any payment. Any credit trimmed by this limit stays claimable for a later purchase.
- **All discounts: 50% per payment.** With a coupon, everything together (coupon, referral discount, referral credit) takes at most **50%** off.

## Referrals don't apply between team members

Referrals are for bringing **new businesses** to [[ app.name ]], not for your own team. If you and the person you referred are on the same team, the referral gives **no benefit to anyone** — no referral discount for them and no reward or credit for either of you. They can still sign up and use the app normally.

You count as team members when any of these is true:

- **You invited them** to your workspace and they signed up with your referral link — for example, a business owner inviting an agent.
- **They invited you** to their workspace, before or after signing up with your code.
- **You're both members of the same workspace** — for example, two agents working in one client's workspace. A suspended member still counts.
- **One of you has a pending or accepted invitation** to a workspace the other belongs to. Leaving the workspace later doesn't change this.
- **They used an invitation link from your workspace** and your referral code together.

When this happens:

- **At sign-up**, the register page tells them the referral code won't apply, and the sign-up doesn't count toward your **Total Uses**.
- **At checkout**, no referral discount is offered. Referral credit earned between the two of you before you became teammates can't be used while you're teammates.
- **When they pay**, no rewards are created and it doesn't count as a **Conversion**.
- You'll get an email when a referral earns you a reward (turn it off in [Account settings](account-settings.md#emails)).
