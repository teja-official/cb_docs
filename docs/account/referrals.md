# Referrals

Share [[ app.name ]] and you both save.

<span class="tj-path">Account › Billing › **Referrals**</span>

## How it works

<div class="steps" markdown>

1. Copy your **referral code** or link from the Referrals page. Everyone has one.
2. Your friend signs up with your link (the code fills in automatically) or types the code at sign-up.
3. They get a **discount on their first paid purchase**.
4. When they pay for the first time, you **both earn a reward** — credit toward a future purchase.

</div>

## Your stats

- **Total Uses** — sign-ups with your code.
- **Conversions** — of those, how many paid.
- **Claimable Rewards** — credit ready to use. It's applied at your next checkout.

## Rules

- A code can be applied at sign-up, or later — but only **before the first purchase**.
- Referral credit covers up to **10%** of an order, and counts toward the 50% cap on all discounts. Any credit trimmed by the cap stays claimable.
- You'll get an email when a referral earns you a reward (turn it off in [Account settings](account-settings.md#emails)).
