# Notifications

[[ app.name ]] keeps you informed in the app (the :lucide-bell: bell), by desktop notification, and by email.

## The bell

Click the bell in the top bar to see recent notifications. Click one to open what it's about. Notifications arrive live — no need to refresh.

### Categories

Choose which categories appear in **Account settings → In-app notifications**:

| Category | Includes |
|---|---|
| **Handoffs** | A lead needs a human agent. |
| **Conversations** | New messages, closed conversations, buying intent. |
| **AI & knowledge base** | Knowledge base ready or failed, low AI confidence. |
| **Billing** | Payments and subscription changes. |
| **Team** | Invitations sent to you and accepted by others. |
| **Lead sources** | Imports and reconnect requests. |

These settings apply in this and every workspace you use.

### Sound and desktop

- **Play a sound** — a short chime when a notification arrives.
- **Desktop notifications** — a system notification when the [[ app.name ]] tab is in the background. Your browser asks for permission the first time.

!!! tip "Not getting desktop notifications?"
    Check that notifications are allowed for [[ app.name ]] in your browser's site settings, and in your operating system's notification settings for the browser.

## Emails

The workspace owner gets lifecycle and alert emails at the **Reporting email**: welcome, subscription confirmed / expiring / expired, payment failed, auto-renew turned off, invoices, referral rewards, lead-source reconnect requests, the daily report, the end-of-day missed activity report, leads ready to buy, and error alerts. Turn the optional ones on or off in [Account settings → Emails](account-settings.md#emails).

## Agent alerts

Human agents get handoff alerts on their own **alert channel** (Telegram, email or SMS) — see [Human agents](../setup/human-agents.md#notification-channels).
