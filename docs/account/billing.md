# Billing & plans

Billing pages are open to every member, but always show **your own** workspace's billing. Only the workspace owner pays for a workspace.

<span class="tj-path">Account › Billing</span>

## Subscription

<span class="tj-path">Billing › **Subscription**</span>

Shows your current plan, **Start Date**, **Billing Cycle**, **Auto-Renew** status and when it renews or ends.

| Notice | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Your plan renews automatically in N days* | Auto-renew is on; you'll be charged on the date shown. |
| *Your plan ends in N days* | Auto-renew is off. Renew now, or turn on auto-renew. |
| *Renew by … to keep your workspace running* | The plan ended; you're in the grace period. |
| *We're retrying your automatic renewal* | A renewal charge failed; access continues until the date shown. |
| **Access paused** | No active plan — the app is locked until you renew. |

### Change plan

- **Upgrade any time** — the unused part of your current plan is credited toward the new one. If the credit covers the whole price, the change is applied without payment.
- **Downgrade** — switch to a smaller plan from the same page.
- **Renew** — extends your plan from its current end date.

### Auto-renew

Auto-renew is **off until you turn it on** — no surprise charges. Turning it on sets up a Razorpay payment mandate; turning it off stops future charges. Automatic renewals never use coupons.

### Cancel

Click **Cancel Subscription**. *Your plan stays active until the end date and won't renew after that — automatic charges stop right away. You'll lose access to the app when it ends; your setup and data are kept.*

### Grace period

If a plan ends without renewal, access continues for **2 days** (**Grace Period**, **Days Remaining**). After that the app locks — Team & Workspace, Account and Billing stay open so you can renew.

## Checkout

<div class="steps" markdown>

1. Choose a plan and billing cycle.
2. Check **Customer Information** (name, email, phone, billing address).
3. Apply a **coupon** if you have one. The welcome offer is pre-applied for eligible new customers — remove it to use a different code. **One coupon per order.**
4. Review the total. Lines can include upgrade credit, coupon, **Referral discount (first purchase)**, referral credit and *Small balance waived*.
5. Pay securely with **Razorpay**.

</div>

The price shown at checkout is exactly what you're charged.

!!! info "Discount limits"
    A single coupon can take at most 50% off, and all discounts together at most 50%. If they add up to more, referral credit is reduced first (the rest stays available for next time).

## Plan Usage

<span class="tj-path">Billing › **Plan Usage**</span>

How much of each plan limit you're using, with **Near Limit** and **Limit Reached** badges:

- **Products (nurtures)**, **Team seats**, **AI model connections**, **Channel connections**, **Handoff agents**, **Custom roles**
- **Meta lead forms**, **Google Ads lead forms**, **Google Sheets connections**, **Spreadsheet imports**
- **New leads per month** — resets monthly on the day your subscription started (also on yearly plans).

Limits on records (campaigns, channels…) don't reset on renewal. Channel connections count every saved channel, active or not.

## Payment History

<span class="tj-path">Billing › **Payment History**</span>

Every order with its number, date, amount and status. Click an order to see its details and pricing breakdown. Invoices are also emailed to the reporting email.
