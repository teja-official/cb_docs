# Help & support

Report a problem, ask a question or suggest an improvement — right from the app. Support is available even if your plan has ended.

<span class="tj-path">Avatar menu › **Help & Support**</span>

## Open a ticket

<div class="steps" markdown>

1. Open **Help & Support** from your avatar menu (or the account sidebar). The page you were on is filled in for you.
2. Click to create a new ticket.
3. Choose a **category** (below). A description template helps you include what we need.
4. Set a **priority**: Low, Medium, High or Urgent.
5. Describe the problem: what you did, what you expected, what happened.
6. Attach up to **5 screenshots** (PNG, JPEG or WebP). They're compressed in your browser without losing quality.
7. Submit. You'll get email updates.

</div>

| Category | Use it for |
|---|---|
| **Bug report** | Something is broken or not working as expected. |
| **Improvement** | Make an existing feature better or easier to use. |
| **Feature request** | Suggest something new the app can't do yet. |
| **Support / Help** | Questions, setup help or how-to guidance. |
| **Billing & payments** | Plans, payments, invoices, coupons or refunds. |
| **Integrations & channels** | Lead sources (Meta, Google Ads, Sheets) or messaging channels. |
| **Other** | Anything else you'd like to tell us. |

## Track your tickets

The list has **Active**, **Resolved & closed** and **All** tabs, a category filter and search. Admins can tick **Only mine** to hide teammates' tickets.

Open a ticket to see its status tracker, timeline, screenshots and replies.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Open** | Received, waiting for our team. |
| **In progress** | We're working on it. |
| **Waiting on you** | We need more information — reply in the ticket. |
| **Resolved** | We think it's fixed. Reply if it isn't and it reopens. |
| **Closed** | Done. You can **reopen** it if needed. |

## Who sees tickets

Tickets stay inside your workspace. **Admins see all of the workspace's tickets**; other members see only their own.
