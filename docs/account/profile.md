# Profile

<span class="tj-path">Account › **Profile**</span>

Your account details and contact information.

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **First Name**, **Last Name** | Shown to teammates and in agent alerts. |
| **Phone Number** | Your contact number. |
| **Email Address** | Your login email. Changing it needs verification (below). |

The page also shows timestamps and account state (when you joined, last login).

## Change your login email

<div class="steps" markdown>

1. Click change next to **Email Address**.
2. Enter the **New email address**. We send a code to it.
3. Enter the **Verification code** to confirm.

</div>

Reports and alerts can go to a different inbox — set the **Reporting email** in [Account settings](account-settings.md#emails).
