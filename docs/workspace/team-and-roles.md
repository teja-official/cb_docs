# Team & roles

<span class="tj-path">Workspace › **Team & Workspace**</span>

Everything in [[ app.name ]] belongs to a **workspace**. You own one workspace (created when you sign up) and can join teammates' workspaces. This page is open even before you have a plan.

## Rename the workspace

Click the workspace name at the top of the page, type a new one and save. The name is used as `{{business_name}}` in messages to leads.

## Members

The **Members** list shows everyone in the workspace with their role. Search by name or email.

### Invite a teammate

<div class="steps" markdown>

1. Click **Invite member**.
2. Enter their **Email** and choose a **Role** (Agent by default).
3. Send the invitation.

</div>

They get an email. Once they accept, they appear in the member list. If they don't have an account yet, they sign up from the link and land straight in your workspace.

- **Pending invitations** are listed below the members. You can **revoke** one any time before it's accepted.
- Pending invitations count toward your plan's **team seats**.
- Inviting needs an active plan and the *Team invites* plan feature.

### Change a role or remove a member

Pick a different role next to a member to change their access. Click :lucide-trash-2: to remove them — they lose access to this workspace immediately; their own workspace isn't affected.

!!! tip "Make someone a handoff agent"
    Being a member doesn't make someone an agent. After they join, add them in [Human agents](../setup/human-agents.md) so they receive handoffs.

## Roles & permissions

*Admin and Agent are built in. Create roles to give teammates exactly the access they need.*

| Role | Access |
|---|---|
| **Admin** | Everything — every permission, always. The workspace owner is an Admin. |
| **Agent** | View leads, conversations, campaigns and dashboards; send messages; take over and release conversations. No configuration access. |
| **Custom roles** | Whatever you choose, permission by permission. |

### Custom roles

<div class="steps" markdown>

1. In **Roles & permissions**, create a role.
2. Enter a **Role name** and a **Description**.
3. Tick the permissions it should have — see the [full list](../help/permissions-reference.md).
4. Save, then assign it to members.

</div>

- Built-in role names can't be changed, but you can still edit their permissions (except Admin, which always has everything).
- A role with members can't be deleted — reassign its members first.
- Custom roles need the *Custom roles & permissions* plan feature, and your plan sets how many you can create.

Permission changes apply immediately — members' menus update live without logging out.

## Other workspaces

- **Invitations for you** — join a teammate's workspace. Accepting switches you into it.
- **Workspaces you've joined** — teammates' workspaces you're a member of. **Leave** removes your access.
- **Switch workspace** with the switcher at the top left of the app. Notifications, permissions and data change to that workspace.

!!! note "Billing belongs to the owner"
    Each workspace's plan is paid by its owner. Billing pages always show **your own** workspace's billing, even when you're working in someone else's.
