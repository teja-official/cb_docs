# FAQ

## Product

??? question "Which ad platforms does it work with?"
    Meta Lead Ads (Facebook and Instagram lead forms) and Google Ads lead forms. Connect your account once and every new lead is picked up automatically. You can also import leads from Google Sheets or a CSV/Excel file.

??? question "How quickly does a lead get a reply?"
    As soon as the lead reaches us, the AI writes and sends the first message. That normally means seconds after the form is submitted, at any hour.

??? question "How does the AI know what to say about my business?"
    You give it a knowledge base: product details, pricing, FAQs and anything else leads usually ask. It answers from that content, in the tone you choose. You can try it in Test Chat before it talks to real leads. [Knowledge base](../nurture-campaigns/knowledge-base.md)

??? question "Will the AI make things up?"
    It's instructed to answer only from your knowledge base and never invent facts, prices, links or contact details. If it isn't sure, and you have agents, it hands the lead to your team.

??? question "What if a lead says "not now" or asks to be contacted later?"
    The AI understands requests like "message me tomorrow morning" or "next week", schedules the follow-up, and comes back on its own at that time. It uses the lead's local time and avoids late-night messages. [Follow-ups](../nurture-campaigns/follow-ups.md)

??? question "What happens if a lead never replies to the first message?"
    The AI follows up on its own, a few times over the next days, on the same channel and outside late-night hours in the lead's local time. The follow-ups stop as soon as the lead replies on any channel or you take over. If the lead stays quiet, the conversation is closed as "no response" so your inbox stays clean.

??? question "Where does the AI talk to leads?"
    On the messaging channels you connect — WhatsApp, Telegram, Email and SMS — plus each lead's personal web chat link. Every conversation, on every channel, shows up in one inbox.

??? question "Does the AI say it's an AI?"
    No — it doesn't describe itself as an AI or explain how it works. It writes as your business.

??? question "What languages does it support?"
    Whatever your AI model supports. Add *"Reply in the language the lead writes in"* to the campaign's **Instructions**.

??? question "Can I sell several products?"
    Yes — create one nurture campaign per product. Leads are routed by deep link, ad form, the product they name, or a product menu.

## Team

??? question "Is it only for businesses with a sales team?"
    No. It works just as well if you sell one product on your own. The AI handles the conversations and tells you when a lead is ready. If you have a team, leads are shared between your agents automatically.

??? question "Can I take over a conversation myself?"
    Yes. You're notified when a lead is ready, can take over any chat from the live inbox, and can hand it back to the AI afterwards. [Conversations](../engage/conversations.md)

??? question "Can I limit what teammates can see or change?"
    Yes. Use the built-in Agent role, or create custom roles permission by permission. [Team & roles](../workspace/team-and-roles.md)

??? question "Can I be in more than one workspace?"
    Yes. Accept invitations from teammates and switch with the workspace switcher. Your own workspace and billing stay separate.

## Billing & data

??? question "Is there a free trial?"
    No. A new workspace needs a plan before the app unlocks. Team & Workspace, your profile and billing work before you pay. [Choose a plan](../getting-started/choose-a-plan.md)

??? question "How does billing work?"
    Pick a monthly or yearly plan and pay securely through Razorpay. Auto-renew stays off unless you turn it on, and you can cancel anytime — you keep access until your paid period ends.

??? question "What if I outgrow my plan?"
    Upgrade any time from Billing. The unused part of what you already paid is credited toward the new plan.

??? question "Do I pay for AI usage separately?"
    Yes — you use your own AI provider key, so tokens are billed by your provider. WhatsApp and Twilio messages are also billed by Meta and Twilio.

??? question "Is my data safe?"
    API keys, tokens and passwords are encrypted before they're stored and are never shown again. Each workspace's data is kept separate.

??? question "What happens to my data if I cancel?"
    You keep access until your paid period ends. After that the app is locked, but your setup and data are kept — renew to pick up where you left off.
