# Manage campaigns

<span class="tj-path">Engage › **Nurture Campaigns**</span>

Each campaign card shows its status, number of **Leads**, **Channels** and **Tone**, and which channel sends the first message (*First message via …*).

## Find a campaign

- **Search campaigns…** by name.
- Filter **Published & drafts / Published / Drafts**, and by status (active or paused).

## Actions

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Edit** / **Continue Editing** | Opens the wizard. Drafts reopen on the step you left. |
| **Activate** | Starts the campaign: the AI answers its leads and follows up. |
| **Deactivate** (pause) | *Leads on this campaign will stop receiving AI follow-up until you reactivate it.* |
| **Delete** | A draft is removed permanently. For a published campaign, all associated leads and conversations lose their nurture reference. |

!!! warning "Pausing stops follow-ups"
    Leads on a paused campaign stop receiving AI follow-up, and pending no-reply follow-ups are stopped. Use it when a product is out of stock or an offer has ended.

## Changing a live campaign

You can edit any step of a published campaign and click **Save Changes**. If you change the knowledge base or instructions, press **Process KB** again so the AI searches the updated content. Your changes can't be saved past the Knowledge Base step until processing succeeds.

## Campaign analytics

Open a campaign's dashboard from the admin [Dashboard](../engage/dashboards.md) (nurture performance table). It has tabs for:

- **Overview** — the campaign's headline numbers.
- **Funnel** — the lead status funnel (*New → Contacted → Engaged → Qualified → Converted*).
- **Channels** — performance by channel.
- **Follow-ups** — upcoming follow-ups, those due in the next 24 hours, and leads waiting for a reply.
- **Trends** — activity over time.
- **KB health** — how many knowledge pieces were indexed (by source), when the knowledge base was last processed, and the short summary used for greetings.
