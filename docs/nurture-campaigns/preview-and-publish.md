# Step 4: Preview & publish

Review the whole campaign, then publish it.

## The summary

| Row | From step |
|---|---|
| **Name**, **Deep Link ID**, **Description** | 1 · Basic info |
| **Tone**, **Channels Selected**, **Primary Followup Channel**, **Follow-ups if no reply** | 2 · AI & channels |
| **Knowledge Base** | 3 · Knowledge base |

Click :lucide-pencil: next to any row to jump back to that step.

## Publish

<div class="steps" markdown>

1. Leave **Activate on publish** on to go live right away — or turn it off to publish the campaign paused.
2. Click **Publish Nurture**.

</div>

Once published, the campaign:

- answers leads who message your channels and pick this product (or arrive via its [deep link](basic-info.md#deep-link-id));
- can be chosen when connecting a [lead source](../setup/lead-sources/index.md);
- appears in **Nurture Campaigns** as <span class="tj-badge tj-badge--success">Active</span> or <span class="tj-badge">Paused</span>.

For a published campaign the button reads **Save Changes**; changes apply to new messages straight away.

!!! note "Plan limit"
    Your plan sets the number of **products (nurtures)** you can have.
