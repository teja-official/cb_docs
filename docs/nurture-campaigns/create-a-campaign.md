# Create a campaign

<span class="tj-path">Engage › **Nurture Campaigns** › **New Nurture**</span>

The wizard has four steps. Each builds on the last.

| Step | What you set | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Basic Info** | Product name, deep link ID, short description, timezone | [Basic info](basic-info.md) |
| **2. AI & Channels** | AI model, tone, handoff thresholds, channels, primary follow-up channel, no-reply follow-ups, agent assignment | [AI & channels](ai-and-channels.md) |
| **3. Knowledge Base** | Knowledge blocks, Q&A, instructions — then **Process KB** and **Test Chat** | [Knowledge base](knowledge-base.md) |
| **4. Preview** | Review everything, then **Publish Nurture** | [Preview & publish](preview-and-publish.md) |

## How saving works

- **Each Next saves the step.** The header shows **Draft · Saved**. You can close the browser and come back — the campaign appears in the list as a draft with **Continue Editing**, and opens on the step you left.
- **Back** returns to the previous step without losing anything. Click a completed step in the progress bar to jump back to it.
- **Next on the Knowledge Base step unlocks only when the knowledge base has been processed** and hasn't changed since. Hover the disabled button to see why.

!!! info "Need help inside the wizard?"
    Click the :lucide-circle-help: help button in the wizard header for *How the Nurture Wizard Works*, and on the Knowledge Base step for *Building a Knowledge Base*.

## Quick start

<div class="steps" markdown>

1. **Basic Info** — type the product name (the deep link ID fills in automatically), add a one-line description and choose your leads' timezone.
2. **AI & Channels** — pick an active AI model and a tone. Select your channels, choose the **primary follow-up channel**, and keep **Follow up automatically when a lead doesn't reply** on. Pick an agent assignment strategy.
3. **Knowledge Base** — fill in the blocks you have information for (pricing, FAQs…), add a few Q&A pairs, press **Process KB**, and ask questions in **Test Chat** until the answers are right.
4. **Preview** — check the summary, leave **Activate on publish** on, and click **Publish Nurture**.

</div>

Then connect a [lead source](../setup/lead-sources/index.md) to this campaign, or share its [deep link](basic-info.md#deep-link-id).
