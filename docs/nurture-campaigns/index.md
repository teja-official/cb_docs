# Nurture campaigns

A **nurture campaign** (or *nurture*) is one product's end-to-end AI follow-up setup. It holds everything the AI needs to sell that product:

- the **knowledge base** — what the AI knows about the product;
- the **AI model** and **tone** it writes in;
- the **channels** it talks on, and which one sends the first message;
- the **follow-up schedule** for leads who don't reply;
- the **handoff rules** and how human agents are chosen.

<span class="tj-path">Engage › **Nurture Campaigns**</span>

!!! tip "One campaign per product"
    Each nurture maps to one product, to keep the AI's context focused. Create a separate campaign for each distinct product or offer — e.g. *Premium Insurance Plan* and *Travel Cover*, not one "Insurance" campaign.

## Before you create one

You need at least one **active** [AI model](../setup/ai-models.md) and one **active** [channel](../setup/channels/index.md). [Human agents](../setup/human-agents.md) are optional but recommended. The campaign page has shortcuts to **Channels**, **AI Models** and **Agents** at the top.
